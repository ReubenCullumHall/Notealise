01What it is

Shape it until it fits how you think.

Spaces keep things separate. Not only your notes, but how they look.

You can customise a space to fit its role in your ecosystem. Research tight and dark; journal soft and wide.

02What it isn’t

Your notes don’t live inside the app.

No account. No server. No format only Notealise can read.

Delete the app and your notes stay exactly where they were.

06Questions

Short answers, no small print.

The things worth knowing before you download.

Is it really free?

Yes. No trial and no subscription.

Why does my computer warn me when I open it?

Notealise isn’t signed with a paid certificate yet, so Windows and macOS ask you to confirm the first time. That’s the only reason. Step-by-step guides for Windows and Mac.

Does it work offline?

Yes. It runs on your computer and only goes online to check for updates, or to fetch an extra font if you pick one. Everything it connects to.

Can I use it on two computers?

There’s no built-in sync. Keep your notes folder in OneDrive, Dropbox or iCloud Drive and open it on each computer, Windows or Mac. Just don’t edit the same note on both at once.

Will my notes open in Obsidian or VS Code?

Yes. They’re ordinary Markdown files, so other apps can open the same folder. A few extras, like coloured text, can look plainer there.

Is there a phone app?

No. It’s built for Windows and for Macs, both Apple silicon and Intel.
