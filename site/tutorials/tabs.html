Tabs and columns

A click in the sidebar opens a note in the tab you’re on. These keep more than one open.

Tabs

Another tab

Ctrl/Cmd + click a note →

Click a note in the sidebar with Ctrl held (Cmd on a Mac) and it opens in a new tab. The strip of tabs shows once there are two.

Go to a note

Ctrl/Cmd + P →

Type part of a note’s name, move with the arrow keys and press Enter. Ctrl/Cmd + Enter opens it in a new tab.

Side by side

Two notes at once

Drop a tab on another tab →

Drag a tab onto the middle of another and the two open side by side, joined as one tab. Up to three fit. Ctrl/Cmd + \ opens an empty column beside the note you’re in.

Move a column

The arrow on its top edge →

Point at a column’s top bar and an arrow shows on its top edge — click it to swap places with the next column. Or drag the column by its top edge.

Take one out

Right-click its name in the joined tab →

Or choose Split them all apart. Dragging its name onto an empty part of the tab strip takes it out too.

Spaces

Change space

Swipe sideways with two fingers →

Or Shift + scroll, Ctrl/Cmd + Alt + Shift + ← or →, or click a space button at the bottom of the sidebar.

Also:

  • Drag the line between two columns to change their width.
  • Ctrl/Cmd + Alt + ← or → moves between tabs.
