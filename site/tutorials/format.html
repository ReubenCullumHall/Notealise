Format bar and / commands

The bar above a note styles the words you have selected. Type / on any line for a list of everything else you can add.

On the bar

Style words

B I U S →

Select the words and press a button, or use the keys: Ctrl/Cmd + B, Ctrl/Cmd + I, Ctrl/Cmd + U and Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + X for strikethrough. Press again to take the style off.

Your four buttons

Click a dashed button →

Pick a command for it. From then on the button runs that command. Change or clear the four in Settings → Look → Shortcuts.

The / menu

Open the list

/ →

Type it at the start of a line or after a space, keep typing to narrow the list, then press Enter. Most commands also answer to a short word, such as /ul, /todo or /h1.

Headings

/h1 →

/h1, /h2 or /h3 — or type # and a space at the start of a line, with ## and ### for the smaller sizes.

Lists

/todo →

/bullet, /numbered or /todo — or type - then a space, 1. then a space, or - [ ] then a space at the start of a line.

On the bar, pressing a heading or list button a second time takes it off again. Typing a / command always adds it.
