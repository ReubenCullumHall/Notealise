Opening Notealise for the first time

Here is how to get past the warning Windows shows for an app it has not seen before.

Notealise is not signed with a Microsoft certificate — I have not bought one yet. That is the only reason the warning appears. The full source is on GitHub.

Ready to install Notealise for Windows. Download it

  1. Open the installer

    Double-click Notealise-Setup.exe in your Downloads folder, or click it in your browser’s download bar.

  2. Click “More info”

    Windows shows a blue box headed Windows protected your PC. At first it only offers Don’t run. Click the small More info link to reveal the rest.

  3. Click “Run anyway”

    The box now names the app and shows a Run anyway button. Click it. Notealise installs and opens, and Windows will not ask again.

Nothing happened when you double-clicked? Some browsers hold the file until you confirm the download. Check the download bar or your Downloads folder and open it from there.
