Okay, so for the biology test on Friday, the main thing to remember is how photosynthesis works. Plants take in carbon dioxide through small holes in their leaves called stomata, and they take in water through their roots. Using energy from sunlight, the chloroplasts turn those into glucose and oxygen. The oxygen is released into the air, and the glucose is used for energy or stored as starch. The rate of photosynthesis depends on three limiting factors: light intensity, carbon dioxide concentration, and temperature.
